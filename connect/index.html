Balance Books Connector

Connect QuickBooks

Balance Books Connector is a private link between your QuickBooks Online company and Balance Books Co. We use it to do your bookkeeping in that company: bills, expenses, journal entries, receipt attachments, and reports.

It is used only by Balance Books Co. It is not sold or shared as a public app.

How authorization works

An admin of your QuickBooks Online company approves the connection on Intuit’s screen. That approval is what allows the connector to access the company. Balance Books cannot connect a company that has not authorized it, and we never ask for your QuickBooks password.

To connect, email alexa@balancebooks.co. We will send the authorization step to an admin of the company. The admin reviews Intuit’s access screen and approves Balance Books Connector.

Client data is handled as described in the privacy policy. The license is the terms of use.
