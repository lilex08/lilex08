Reconnect QuickBooks

If a connection to Balance Books Connector has expired or was disconnected, an admin of the QuickBooks Online company can authorize it again.

Email Alexa Madden at alexa@balancebooks.co. She will send the authorization step. Until an admin approves it on Intuit’s screen, Balance Books cannot access the company. We never ask for your QuickBooks password.

A reconnected company is covered by the same privacy policy and terms of use. The steps are the same as a first connection, described on the connect page.
