The QuickBooks Online connection to Balance Books Connector has been disconnected.

Balance Books deletes the access tokens for that company. The connector can no longer reach the company’s QuickBooks data. Disconnecting does not delete the books stored in QuickBooks Online. Those records stay with the company and with Intuit.

To connect again, email Alexa Madden at alexa@balancebooks.co. An admin of the company will need to authorize the connector. You can also open the reconnect page.
