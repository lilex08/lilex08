Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Balance Books Co. is a sole proprietorship based in Quebec, Canada. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with Balance Books Connector and this website.

Balance Books Connector is a private integration. Balance Books Co. uses it to do bookkeeping in its own clients’ QuickBooks Online companies. It is not sold or distributed to the public.

Information we access

When an admin of a QuickBooks Online company authorizes the connector, we access that company’s accounting data through Intuit’s QuickBooks Online API. The data we use for the work can include bills, expenses, journal entries, receipts and other attachments, reports, and the company and transaction details those tasks require. Names and contact details of vendors, customers, or employees may be included when they appear on those records.

We access a company only with that admin’s authorization. We do not bypass Intuit’s consent screen, and we do not ask for a QuickBooks password.

This website does not require an account. It does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The temperature shown in the header comes from a public weather request for Montreal and does not identify you. The email button copies our address to your clipboard only if you use it.

Why we use it

We use QuickBooks data only to provide bookkeeping services to the client who connected the company. That work is entering bills, expenses, and journal entries, attaching receipts, and running reports.

We do not sell personal information or QuickBooks data. We do not share it with other businesses, data brokers, or advertisers, and we do not use it for marketing.

Who can see it

Access is limited to Balance Books Co. We store connection tokens in encrypted form.

The client’s books remain in QuickBooks Online, which Intuit operates under its own terms. We do not give client data to anyone else for their own use.

AI assistance

An AI assistant may operate the connector on Balance Books Co.’s behalf. It works only under the control and direction of Balance Books Co., and only to perform the client’s bookkeeping. The assistant does not acquire any right to the data. We do not allow client data to be sold, or to be used to train public models.

How long we keep it

We keep connection tokens, and any working copies we hold for an engagement, only while that QuickBooks company stays connected and we are engaged to keep the books.

When the company is disconnected, we delete the connection tokens. The connector can no longer access that company. We also delete the working copies we hold for that connection, unless the law requires us to keep a specific record. If it does, we keep only what that duty requires, and we delete it when the duty ends.

Disconnecting does not delete the books stored in the client’s QuickBooks Online company. Those records stay with the client and Intuit.

Your choices

A company admin can disconnect Balance Books Connector at any time in QuickBooks Online, or by emailing us. You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You may withdraw consent to our use of your personal information. If a legal duty requires us to keep something, we will say so.

Quebec Law 25 and PIPEDA

We handle personal information under Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, including the requirements known as Law 25, and under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies.

The owner of Balance Books Co. is the person responsible for the protection of personal information. You can reach them at the email below.

You may also contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
